## Reworked abilities

- **#10 — First Blood:** genuine PvP kill gives Speed I + Regeneration I for 15 seconds. 30-second cooldown. This base kill-rush carries upward.
- **#9 — Featherstep:** 25% less fall damage.
- **#8 — Relentless:** 15% less sprint movement exhaustion/hunger; keeps First Blood as ranks climb.
- **#7 — Steadfast:** 10% knockback resistance.
- **#6 — Hunter's Recovery:** genuine PvP kill restores 2.5 hearts. 30-second cooldown.
- **#5 — Berserker:** below 40% health gives Speed I + Strength I + 3 absorption hearts for 15 seconds. 30-second cooldown. Visual/sound activation cue.
- **#4 — Revenge:** a significant PvP hit refunds 15% of the lost health and grants Resistance I for 15 seconds. 30-second cooldown. Below 50% health also grants 4 absorption hearts for 15 seconds on a 45-second cooldown. Visual/sound cue.
- **#3 — Blood Feast:** genuine PvP kill restores 3 hearts, grants 4 absorption hearts for 15 seconds, and grants Strength I for 30 seconds. Red damage-indicator particle burst + crit sound.
- **#2 — Second Wind:** below 30% health grants Speed II + Resistance II + Regeneration III for 20 seconds. 60-second cooldown. Blue soul-fire particle burst + chime.
- **#1 — King's Wrath:** below 35% health grants Strength II + Resistance II for 20 seconds. 60-second cooldown. Genuine PvP kill still restores 5 hearts on a 30-second cooldown. Gold/orange flame burst + activation sound.

Lower passive unlocks still carry upward where appropriate. Same-family kill-heal upgrades do not stack duplicate heals.

## Visual identity

Top-5 ability activations now include a short vanilla-particle burst and vanilla sound cue. These are server-side effects and require no client mod or resource pack.
